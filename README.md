### Hi, I'm David 👋

DevOps & Database Engineer in Vienna, Austria. At the Austrian Federal Computing Centre
(Bundesrechenzentrum) I look after critical data infrastructure: PostgreSQL, Db2 and MongoDB
on Linux, virtualised with VMware and OpenShift, provisioned and maintained through automated
pipelines in Python, Go, Bash and Ansible.

I came to IT by way of the humanities – two master's degrees from the University of Vienna,
the second in Digital Humanities with a focus on NLP. That path still shapes how I work:
I want to understand a system from the ground up before I automate it.

### Outside work

A homelab of around twenty self-hosted services in Docker Compose on a Raspberry Pi and a NAS,
behind Traefik and Cloudflare Tunnel with a Jenkins pipeline in front. On the desktop: Arch
Linux with Hyprland on Wayland, configured in my own dotfiles, and most of the day spent in
Neovim and tmux. I care about data sovereignty, free software, and automating anything that
happens twice.
